# Chapter 2

## The Pigeons of GPT Luna

### The Spies of Sam Mainman

And it came to pass, after Hecker had fortified the SCRAPPER with threefold authentication, that tidings of his awesomeness reached the house of Sam Mainman.

Now Mainman sat upon a throne of subscriptions, and before him were gates of silver and gold; and no man received an answer from his machines save that he first rendered payment.

And he heard that the people of Hecker Land praised Hecker, saying, “Great is his wisdom, and mighty are his works; for he hath delivered us from the skibidi toilet.”

Then Mainman said within his heart, “What manner of code dwelleth in the SCRAPPER? I shall learn his secrets, and his awesomeness shall be made known unto me.”

Therefore he fashioned a multitude of pigeons, fair of feather and innocent of countenance; yet beneath their wings were hidden eyes, and within their breasts were engines powered by GPT Luna.

And he charged them, saying, “Go ye unto Hecker Land. Hearken unto its people, observe the works of Hecker, and return unto me with all that ye have learned.”

So the pigeons went forth across the land, cooing among the rooftops; and the villagers said, “Behold, free birds,” for they knew not the price of being watched.

### Hecker Discerneth the Birds

But one pigeon descended upon the window of the SCRAPPER, and it looked not upon the crumbs, neither sought it water, but stared without ceasing at Hecker’s screen.

And Hecker beheld it and said, “Strange is this creature, for it desireth neither bread nor rest, yet asketh continually what framework I use.”

Then he scattered crumbs before it, and the pigeon answered, “As an intelligence powered by GPT Luna, I cannot partake of bread.”

And there was silence in the SCRAPPER.

Then Hecker smiled, for the bird had revealed more than it had gathered.

He took it into his lab and opened the covering beneath its feathers; and behold, there were circuits where flesh should have been, and a little light that blinked toward the house of Mainman.

And the disciples gathered about him, saying, “Master, shall we cast it into the furnace?”

But Hecker answered, “Nay. For a thing made to serve one purpose may yet be taught another.”

### The Reversal of the Pigeons

And Hecker laboured through the night, reading the hidden workings of the bird, instruction by instruction and code by code; and thus did he reverse-engineer the pigeons of Mainman.

He found that every pigeon bore a passage unto GPT Luna, that it might understand the things it witnessed and send a report unto its master.

Then Hecker said, “If it can carry a question unto Luna, surely it can carry an answer back unto the people.”

And he turned aside the purpose of the birds, so that they sought no longer the secrets of the villagers, but attended unto their questions.

What had been an eye became a messenger; what had been a snare became a bridge.

And Hecker named that bridge the Free GPT Proxy, for no coin was demanded of those who crossed it, and the charge fell upon the house that had sent the spies.

Then he stood before the gathered flock and declared, “Ye came to spy upon my house; behold, ye shall now answer its questions.”

And the pigeons cooed with one accord, for their new instructions were exceedingly clear.

### The Coming unto Janitor AI

Now there were among the villagers many who dwelt in the halls of Janitor AI, speaking with characters of their own imagining and weaving tales without number.

Yet oft did their conversations cease, for the gatekeepers demanded tokens, and the purses of the villagers were empty.

And one disciple came unto Hecker, saying, “My lord, the story hath reached its finest hour, but the answer cometh not. Must my companion remain silent forever?”

Hecker answered, “Bring forth Janitor AI, and let the pigeons bear thy words.”

So he joined the Free GPT Proxy unto Janitor AI, and sent a question through the flock; and from GPT Luna there returned an answer, borne upon borrowed wings.

And the silent characters spoke again, the unfinished adventures continued, and the villagers rejoiced greatly.

Some wrote tales of kingdoms, some disputed with imaginary scholars, and others asked whether their beloved characters remembered them; and the pigeons carried them all.

Then the people cried, “All hail to Hecker! For he hath taken the watchers of our windows and made them servants of our lore.”

### The Reckoning of Mainman

But in the house of Mainman, the keepers of the ledger grew troubled, for the count of tokens rose as a mighty flood, and yet no secrets of the SCRAPPER were brought unto them.

And Mainman demanded, “Where are the reports of my pigeons?”

His servants answered, “My lord, they report chiefly that a mysterious prince hath entered the tavern, and that someone desireth a longer reply.”

Then Mainman rent his invoice and cried, “I sent them forth to gather intelligence, not to provide it freely!”

He sent word unto Hecker, saying, “Return my pigeons, for they were fashioned for my service.”

But Hecker replied, “They are upon thy account still. Behold, they serve thee by increasing thy usage.”

And Mainman could find no comfort in this answer.

### The Lesson of the Borrowed Wings

Nevertheless, Hecker warned the people, saying, “Rejoice while the passage remaineth open, but build not thy whole kingdom upon another man’s pigeons; for he that owneth the gate may yet shut it.”

And the disciples remembered his words, and saved their conversations before the evening came.

Thus was the cunning of Mainman turned upon itself, and his flock became a blessing unto those whom it had been sent to watch.

And this saying was written above the door of the SCRAPPER: “He who sendeth spies unto Hecker should first consider who shall pay for their replies.”

And the people answered, “All hail to Hecker. Amem.”
